What one-click setup does

Adds any missing starter subject/curriculum records, adds default weekly requirements only where none exist, and keeps existing subject requirements, teacher assignments, and published timetables. It selects teachers using known subject assignments/qualifications where possible, balances workload when expertise is not documented, then generates conflict-checked drafts. Review assignments and every timetable draft before publishing.
This is an editable starter curriculum, not a guarantee of compliance with every CBSE subject combination or your school's specific periods. Add class divisions in Classes & Divisions first. Standard 11–12 classes must have their stream selected below. The timetable optimizer prevents teacher/class period conflicts; it cannot account for unrecorded room, holiday, staffing, or school-specific constraints.
